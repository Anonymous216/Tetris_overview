About Game:

What Is TETRIS?

It's the addictive puzzle game that started it all! By embracing our universal desire to create order out of chaos, the Tetris® game provides intellectual sport that combines continuous fun with mental stimulation. As one of the most recognizable and influential video game brands in the world, it's no wonder why there are hundreds of millions of Tetris products being played, worn, and enjoyed by fans in their everyday lives. For almost forty years, the game and brand have truly transcended the barriers of culture and language, resulting in a fun and exciting playing experience for everyone, everywhere!

Game Mechanics


Tetris is a fast paced, grid based puzzle game played on a 10×20 matrix where seven distinct geometric blocks called Tetrominoes fall continuously from top to bottom, requiring players to rotate, shift, and drop them to fill complete horizontal rows. Completing a row clears those blocks, drops the stack above, and awards points that increase with multi-line clears, T-Spins, and higher difficulty levels until the stack reaches the top border and triggers a Game Over.

Game Rules:

The Tetriminos

7 distinct shapes (I, O, T, L, J, S, Z) each made of 4 blocks.

Controls

Move pieces left or right, rotate them to fit, use Soft Drop to speed up falling, or Hard Drop to instantly place them.

Clearing Lines

Filling a full row clears it from the board. Clearing 4 lines at once is called a Tetris.

Levels & Speed

The game speeds up as you clear more lines, forcing faster decisions.

Game Over

The game ends when blocks stack up to the top of the grid and no space remains for new pieces.

Score

Clear more lines to earn a higher score and get more points.

Game Modes:

Standard Guidelines Modes:

Marathon Mode

Marathon: Stack and clear lines as the game speed progressively increases, aiming to survive as long as possible.

Sprint (40 Lines):

Sprint (40-Line): Race against the clock to clear a total of 40 lines as fast as possible.

Ultra Mode

A score-attack mode with a strict time limit (usually 3 minutes) to get the highest score.

Master/Speed Mode:

Extreme gravity where pieces drop instantly, designed for expert players.

Multiplayer & Battle Royale Modes:

Versus / Battle:

Compete against opponents by clearing lines to send garbage rows to their boards.

Battle Royale (e.g., Tetris 99):

Mass multiplayer survival where you fight 98 other players simultaneously and target specific opponents.

Score Attack:

A head to head match focusing strictly on the highest point score rather than offensive garbage routing.
